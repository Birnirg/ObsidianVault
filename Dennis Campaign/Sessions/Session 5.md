## Session notes

We set up for the night in the Bunk lodge and go to sleep, we dont set up any watch at all. During the night Frank notices some sound and wakes us up. The Urdafhan found us and set up an ambush outside the house, they tried to smoke us out.  we break the door and push outside. We fight the 2 Urdafhans, one gets pushed into the well. and the other runs away. 

we move our sleeping area to the cantina. In the morning we are woken up by the Froblins again who are here to burn the houses. The Froblins ritual cast fireball to burn down the cabins and we move on. 

We discuss the next moves and decide to move South East, which is the same way the Froblins are going. 

we move into the warrens, and at a long distance we see some ruins where someone is blowing something up and moving stones. 

we sit down for some lunch and Ruen sends Corbin to scout the Goblin camp in the warrens. 

Scouting the camp we see that the Kobolds are working for a Red dragonkin who is instructing them in building a stone ring that seems to be a holding/banishing circle for a creature called Nayad. 

The kobolds, have caputured the goblins wives in a prison in the middle of the camp where they are using them as workforce and forcing their men to do their bidding. 

Nayad are protected spirits of water, the bigger and stronger body of water, the stronger the spirit. 

Nayad are the lowest rank of fey/spirit
